Key Charms privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026. Applies to Key Charms 1.0.0.

Summary

Key Charms does not automatically collect personal data and does not sell it. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no internet access: the app does not request Android's Internet permission. Car communication happens over Bluetooth between your phone and your own car. You can choose to export diagnostics through another app as described below.

What stays on your phone

  • Your car's Bluetooth identity (its Bluetooth name and address) so the app can reconnect to it.
  • Your car's VIN, which you type in. It is needed to sign commands for your car and to recognise your car's Bluetooth name. It never leaves the phone.
  • The phone key: a private key generated on your phone and protected by Android's secure key storage. Only its public half is sent to your car during pairing, and only over Bluetooth.
  • Your car-look choice (body and paint colour for the widgets).
  • Car status shown in the widgets (locked/unlocked, doors, battery level, temperatures) as last read from your car over Bluetooth, and your widget settings.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. We have no servers that could receive it.

Permissions and Bluetooth pairing

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): to connect to your car and send it the lock, unlock, frunk, trunk and climate commands you ask for.
  • Companion device pairing: Android's own pairing sheet lets you choose your car once. Key Charms then learns when your car is nearby so a widget can refresh its status. Android does this on the app's behalf; the app never scans the surroundings for other devices.
  • Location: not used. Key Charms does not request any location permission and never reads your location.
  • Internet: not used. The app does not request Android's Internet permission, so it cannot send anything over Wi-Fi or mobile data. Bluetooth is used only to talk to your own car.

Diagnostics

Nothing is collected automatically. If you tap "Share diagnostics" in Settings, the app prepares a short text report (app version, phone model, recent app log) with your VIN and your car's Bluetooth name and address removed, and hands it to the sharing app you pick. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to send it.

Deleting your data

"Remove key" deletes the phone key but keeps the saved car identity, status and preferences. Uninstall the app to delete all of its local data. The app is excluded from Android cloud backup and phone-to-phone transfer. Reports you already shared through another app are not deleted by these actions. Neither removing the key in the app nor uninstalling removes it from your car: delete it in your car's key settings (on Tesla vehicles: Controls › Locks).

Children

Key Charms is intended for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children.
